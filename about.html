about kai

I treat my work and process like an act of archiving. Focusing on how I can document personal experiences and certain phases of my life to a physical form. I have been strictly doing traditional media for quite some time with the exception of digital painting if and when I’m restricted from working with specific materials. I find myself enjoying the physicality of drawing, crafting, and painting. My process involves finding anything I can get my hands on and “trusting the process," working with what you have is my motto. Although most of my work is traditional, I am open to trying all sorts of media at least once! I think because I'm taking the first steps into my art journey, there's no doubt that I'm filled with uncertainty but I hope to continue pursuing human made art. I want my viewers to notice that my work is relatable, that they're highly capable to produce their own works or even go beyond that. I want my audience to understand that art has no limits and no rules.


Kai Ke was born and raised in Socal, Long Beach. Graduated from Long Beach City College with an Associate Degree for Transfer in June 2026. They are currently attending San Jose State University and majoring in Studio Art Practice. They’re currently exploring new media art and the digital world. They focus on showing that art isn't solely tied to technical skills but as a universal language that speaks louder than words.
